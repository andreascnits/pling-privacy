## Common questions

**A reminder didn't arrive.** Open Settings → Reliability check and run the self-test. It sends a real notification and shows how long it took, and lists anything on your phone that can block reminders (notification permission, Focus, battery saving).

**Reminders don't break through Focus (iPhone).** Pling uses Time Sensitive notifications. Make sure "Time Sensitive Notifications" is allowed for Pling under Settings → Notifications → Pling, and that Pling is not silenced in your Focus settings.

**I bought Pling Pro on another device / reinstalled.** Open Settings → Pling Pro → Restore purchase, signed in with the same App Store / Google account. Pro is a one-time purchase and never expires.

**How do I stop a nag?** Tap Done on the notification or in the app. Snooze postpones it; it keeps nagging until it is done or the chain ends.

**Where is my data?** Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud sync. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).
